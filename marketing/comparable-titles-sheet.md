# Comparable Titles Sheet — The High-Agency AI Worker

## Purpose
This sheet identifies comparison territory for publisher conversations. These comps are meant to clarify market position, not imply imitation.

## Positioning summary
This project sits at the intersection of:
- practical AI/workflow guidance,
- business/career strategy,
- and thoughtful technology/culture nonfiction.

It is strongest where **usefulness + intellectual seriousness + human stakes** overlap.

---

## Comparable title 1
### *Co-Intelligence* — Ethan Mollick
**Why it is relevant**
- Serious, accessible writing about how people work with AI
- Broad professional readership
- Strong bridge between practical use and bigger implications

**How this project differs**
- **The High-Agency AI Worker** is more explicitly workflow-centered and more directly focused on preserving judgment, standards, and professional distinctiveness.

---

## Comparable title 2
### *The Coming Wave* — Mustafa Suleyman
**Why it is relevant**
- Strong public-interest AI positioning
- Appeals to readers interested in technological change and large-scale implications

**How this project differs**
- This project is less macro-political and more immediately practical for everyday knowledge work.
- It is more focused on what a reader should do on Monday morning, not only what society should worry about over the next decade.

---

## Comparable title 3
### *Deep Work* — Cal Newport
**Why it is relevant**
- Strong shelf presence in productivity / attention / work quality
- Resonates with readers who want standards and meaningful capability, not just speed

**How this project differs**
- This project addresses the AI-shaped workplace directly and treats AI as a force that must be integrated without eroding depth.

---

## Comparable title 4
### *Atomic Habits* — James Clear
**Why it is relevant**
- Widely successful as practical behavior-change nonfiction
- Appeals to readers seeking repeatable systems rather than abstract motivation

**How this project differs**
- This project is not a habit book, but it shares a systems mindset and applies that mindset to AI-assisted work, judgment, and workflow design.

---

## Optional additional comp territory
Depending on submission strategy, this project may also be described as adjacent to:
- books on modern knowledge work,
- thoughtful creativity/business books,
- AI-and-society nonfiction,
- and intelligent productivity writing.

## Comparative takeaway
If a publisher asks where this belongs, the clearest answer is:

> It combines the practical usefulness of a modern work book, the systems clarity of productivity nonfiction, and the reflective seriousness of an AI-and-society book — but keeps its center on professional judgment.
